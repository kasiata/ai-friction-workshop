A · TRANSPARENT EXTRACTION

What occurs, and what occurs together?

Word frequency counts occurrences. Co-occurrence counts pairs within five content words in the same sentence, at most once per pair per sentence. We examine the 32 most frequent content words. Occurring together does not imply a causal relationship.
